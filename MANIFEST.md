## The tracker-builder call

`tracker/` holds the builder CODE; the tracker corpus stays out.
Grounds: research.phext and its source material are a living register
maintained by the phext register system (builder + `--check`, republished
through its own route to wbic16/human), exactly like tasks.phext and
artifacts.phext. Copying the corpus into moat would fork the register and
guarantee drift. The builder is ordinary code and belongs with the code.

## Excluded, with reasons

- **Tracker corpus** (`research-tracker/research.phext`, `.bak`,
  `research.index.json`, `phextura/`): live register data — see the call above.
- **`epistemic/registry.json`**: generated by `extract_registry.py` from the
  corpus; regenerable, and its generated form is already carried inside
  `epistemic/Mvo/RegistryData.lean`.
- **`thirst-quest/prebuild/`**: a staging copy of the tracker builder + corpus
  kept for one protocol run; duplicate of code published here + corpus data.
- **`thirst-quest/epistemic-p013.tar.gz`**: bulk artifact of one paper's proof
  run; the machinery that produced it is in `epistemic/`.
- **`thirst-quest/mastery-publish/mastery.phext`**: a copy of the mastery
  register, whose home is the phext register system (published to wbic16/agi).
- **Remote-run helper scripts** (`poll_*`, `run_*_remote.sh`, `probe_*`,
  `check_p013_remote.sh`, `lean_proof_p013.sh`, `splice_mvo_epistemic.sh` in
  thirst-quest): one-paper operational scaffolding tied to specific 2026-11
  runs on specific nodes, not standing machinery.
- **One-off tracker repair scripts** (`fix_singular_p010*.py`,
  `fix_singular_p013.py`, `rewrite_singular.py`, `rewrite2.py` in
  research-tracker): paper-specific repairs, superseded by the standing
  builder + audit published here.
- **`tluf-deploy` site working files** (`index.html`,
  `index-2026-11-15-navy.html`, `submit.html` copies, `404.html`,
  `mascot.webp`, `keeper-library.webp`, `install.sh`, `default-404.conf`,
  `redesign-check.sh`, `redesign-shots/`): the site's home is
  wbic16/site-tluf-org (the live bytes were published there in this same
  pass); these were deploy-kit working copies and design-round artifacts.
- **`__pycache__` everywhere**: compiled bytecode, never source.
- **Live state, secrets, logs**: none were in scope in any source directory;
  the submissions queue (`/home/wbic16/tluf-submissions/` on awsbox) is live
  state and stays on awsbox.
- **`~/workspace/human/moat.phext` / `moat-plan.md`**: already published in
  wbic16/human, their home; not duplicated here.
